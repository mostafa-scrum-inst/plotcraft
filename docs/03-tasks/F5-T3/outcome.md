### FS

- **Lens shift (TL ruling 2, A-12).**
  - `PC.cam.viewOffset(W, H, occl)` returns `[W + o, H, o, 0, W, H]`, or null. `visibleAspect` gives
    `(W − o) / H`.
  - `scene.js` `lens()` sets `aspect = (W + o) / H` plus `setViewOffset`, or clears the offset. It runs on resize, on
    `setOcclusion` and on context restore.
  - Poses, the pivot, the finder and the clamps are unchanged. Reset, `rMax` and the tour plan fit the radius with
    the visible aspect.
  - `o` is `canvas.right − panel.left` when the panel is expanded and overlapping: 336 px at 1280x800, and 0 when
    side by side or collapsed.
  - The mock whitelists `setViewOffset`, and `projectPx` matches r128's `Vector3.project` to within 1e-6 px.
  - The canvas has its own `ResizeObserver`, so the renderer follows the 640x360 panel toggle.
- **F1 items (TL ruling 4).**
  - Toasts that quote a measurement are dropped on a unit change.
  - When saving stops, the PM toast shows once per session, together with the note and the chip.
  - **Read-only resume:** `canRead` / `canWrite`. Resume is offered, nothing is written after the probe, and Start
    new still asks.
  - **B-1 fix:** `available = canRead && canWrite`, so write-only storage behaves as in F1.
  - The no-save chip shows on every stage whenever saving is unavailable. A tap shows the PM toast.
- **2D labels.**
  - **Area label:** on one line; on two lines when it is wider than the footprint less 8 px (number / unit, so
    "460" / "sq ft" after B-2); or outside on the street side.
  - **A-15:** overridden edges are culled normally, with a 6 px tick at the midpoint when culled. The street edge is
    always labelled.
  - The legend toggles, and the pool note in the PM format.
- **Stair handle (TL ruling 5).** The hit order was already correct. `plans-edit.spec` now asserts it.
- **TOUR.** The Designer's two numbers are committed (0e84a6d), with the `tourplan` goldens regenerated:
