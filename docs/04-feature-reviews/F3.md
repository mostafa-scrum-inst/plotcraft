### Findings

**1. Bug: removing a room, or moving it to another level, makes the autosaved draft unrestorable; the project is silently lost on the next load (owner FS; fix now, before this batch's republish).**
- **Cause:** `PC.draft.validState` → `plansOk` (draft.js, `where[r.id] !== id`) requires every room of every stored plan to exist on that plan's level. That held in F2, when no draft contained plans with rooms.
- **Since F3-T1, plans are stored per level and kept while stale** (A-7: a stale plan is regenerated on the next entry to stage 3, not at the change). After a room is deleted or moved to another level, the stale plan still lists it:
  - `PC.draft.serialize` writes the draft;
  - `PC.draft.parse` rejects it;
  - `ui.start.init` then calls `removeDraft()` ("corrupted or unknown format: discarded silently").
- **Reproduced:**
  - In node: sample + sync, `propose(removeRoom r5)` → `validState` false, `parse` null.
  - In the built page at 1280x800: Load sample, stage 2, remove a room through the same `propose` the Delete button uses, wait for the autosave, reload. No Resume card, and `localStorage` is empty.
  - In my probe, 14 % of all states hit this.
- **Who is affected:** anyone who edits the room list and closes or reloads the tab before going back to stage 3, which is normal on a phone. That includes the client, who is testing the published build now with the guide (steps on the room list).
- **Why the suites missed it:** they reload only after stage-3 visits or non-room changes. My F2 probe checked exactly this round trip, but in F2 no plan had rooms yet. The F1 lesson applies again: the draft round trip must be attacked by every operation, and every time a new kind of stored data appears.
- **Fix, recommended (TL confirms):** in `plansOk`, require only well-formed, unique room ids in `rooms` and `unmet`, not membership of the level.
  - Whether a plan belongs to its level is already decided by the signature alone (A-7), and the signature contains the level's room ids, so a fresh plan cannot list a foreign room.
  - This keeps a useful property: moving a room away and back restores the old signature, and with it the old plan with its edits.
  - No schema bump.
  - A normaliser that deletes such plans at commit is the alternative. I advise against it: it adds a second staleness mechanism next to the signature.
- **Tests:**
  - unit (`draft.test` / `plans-sync.test`): after `removeRoom`, `updateRoom` to another level and `removeLevels` (move and delete) on the synced sample, `parse(serialize(s))` returns `s` exactly; a forged fresh plan with a foreign id is still rejected or regenerated;
  - e2e (`rooms.spec` or `r5.spec`): sample → stage 2 → Delete a room → reload → Resume is offered and restores exactly → stage 3 regenerates only that level;
  - QA: add room-list operations and the exact draft round trip to the 2 400-operation invariant run in `f3-t2-edit.mjs`.
