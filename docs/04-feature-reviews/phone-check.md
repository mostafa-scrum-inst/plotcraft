# PlotCraft — phone check (about 10 minutes)

Please open the published PlotCraft page **on your phone** (any recent iPhone or Android, normal browser). Hold the phone upright unless a step says otherwise. For each step, reply with **OK** or say what happened instead (a screenshot helps). If a step fails, just carry on with the next one.

## Part A — drawing the plot (stage 1)

1. **Open the page.**
   Good: the page loads within a few seconds and shows a squared grid with a header "PlotCraft" and steps 01–05 across the top. Nothing is cut off at the sides and the page does not scroll sideways.
2. **Draw a shape.** Tap four corners of a rough rectangle on the grid, then tap the first corner again.
   Good: each tap places a point; the shape closes and fills in; the edge lengths and the area appear.
3. **Pinch and move the grid.** Pinch with two fingers to zoom in and out, then drag with two fingers.
   Good: only the grid zooms and moves. The page itself does not zoom or scroll, and no extra point appears.
4. **Remember my work.** Tap "Clear" (confirm), then "Load sample project". Now reload the page.
   Good: after the reload the page offers "Resume draft". If it instead shows a small note "drafts not saved on this device", please tell us; that is also acceptable, we only need to know which.
5. **Dialog blocks the page.** Tap "Start new".
   Good: a box asks you to confirm. While it is open, tapping the page behind it does nothing. Tap "Cancel".

## Part B — the questions (stage 2), with the sample loaded

6. **Go to the questions.** Tap "Next" (or step 02).
   Good: a small drawing of the plot at the top with the building in it; questions below; a "Summary" bar and Back / Next at the bottom, all visible without scrolling the bar away.
7. **Choose the street edge.** With "Street" selected above the drawing, tap a different boundary of the plot.
   Good: the drawing turns so the boundary you tapped is at the bottom, labelled "front". (Tap the original bottom edge again to put it back.)
8. **Switch back / side by tapping.** Tap "Back / side" above the drawing, then tap the left or right boundary.
   Good: its label changes between "side" and "back", with a "*" meaning "set by you", and the shaded setback area changes. Tapping the bottom (street) edge shows a short message that it is always the front.
9. **Switch back / side from the list.** Scroll to the street-edge question (Q6), tap "Back / side of each edge" to open the list, and tap "Make side" (or "Make back") on one of them.
   Good: the button is easy to hit, the edge is highlighted in the drawing, and its label changes.
10. **Footprint slider.** Find "Building footprint % of plot" (Q3) and slowly drag the slider left and right.
    Good: the slider follows your finger smoothly; the building in the drawing and the footprint figure in the summary follow it, or catch up within about a second after you let go. Tell us if it stalls for longer.
11. **Footprint slider on a complicated plot (optional, 2 minutes).** Go back to stage 1, clear the plot and draw a jagged shape with about 10–12 corners, then repeat step 10.
    Good: the same as step 10; a pause of a second or two after letting go is acceptable, please tell us how long.
12. **Type a number with the phone keyboard.** In "Setbacks" (Q2), tap the "Front" number, type 4 and press the keyboard's Go / Done / Enter key.
    Good: the keyboard is the number keypad; the value becomes 4.0 m and the drawing updates. Nothing else on the page jumps or gets stuck behind the keyboard.
13. **Add a room.** Open the "Rooms" tab. Pick a type and a floor from the dropdowns, then tap "Add room".
    Good: the dropdowns open your phone's normal picker; the new room appears in the list for that floor with its name and area, nothing is cut off.
14. **Remove a floor that has rooms.** Open the "Building" tab and change "Number of floors" to 1. Before confirming, try tapping or scrolling the page behind the box that appears.
    Good: a box asks whether to move the rooms or delete them. The page behind does not react. Tap "Cancel": the number of floors goes back.
15. **Remove-floor box with a dropdown open.** On the "Rooms" tab, open a room's floor dropdown (the phone picker is showing), close it without changing anything, then go to "Building" and change the floors to 1 again.
    Good: the same box appears normally, on top, and the earlier picker does not reappear or block it. Tap "Cancel".
16. **Turn the phone sideways** on the questions screen.
    Good: everything still fits; the drawing and the questions are usable; Back / Next stay visible.
