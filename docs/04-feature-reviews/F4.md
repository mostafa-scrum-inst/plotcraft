**Accepted limits to restate to the client** (from the outcomes, in his words)
- **The default 3D view looks from the street.** The pool sits behind the house and appears after one drag; the garden seating shows at the back corner.
- **"Way in" warnings (CD-5)** treat any patch of hall as a way in, even a closed pocket that does not connect to the stairs or the entrance.
- **Large gardens:** at most 120 trees and 200 shrubs (about 1 020 m² of planting) are drawn, so very large gardens look thinner than the % asked, with no message.
- **The sun is approximate,** not real solar data, as labelled in the app.
- **Raising the vegetation % can swap some shrubs for trees;** existing trees never move.
- **A full 10-room floor on a large plot is drawn smaller on a phone** (13.2 px/m). Every room still has its label.
- **Rooms in 3D are told apart by floor colour, not by name labels.** Names show in the room finder and on the tour caption (F5).

  - the real/mock routing in `tests/helpers/browser.mjs`, which counts real-r128 `THREE.` warnings as errors (H2).
  - The 3D layer is 1 100 lines over 10 files, the largest being `scene.js` at 299. My F3 hand-off conditions H1–H6 are all in place:
    - the mock comes first and is the API guard;
    - real r128 is vendored with its sha512 recorded and is routed only on the exact cdnjs URL;

  - Mine: `node tests/run-all.mjs --unit --three` passed all steps (unit 32 files, three 13 files) in 16.9 s.

- **Random-operation probe on real r128** (my scratchpad; nothing in the tree touched):
  - **Setup:** the built page with the vendored r128. 300 random operations at 360x640 touch, dpr 3, and 300 at 1280x800. Operations:
    - level selector, Roof, look (hour and season);
    - room select and fly-to, Reset;
    - tour start, pause, resume, stop and seek;
    - rebuilds through Q10 / Q18 / Q19 commits, stage 4 → 5 close and reopen;
    - `WEBGL_lose_context` lose and restore (18 times).
  - **Invariants after each operation:**
    - meshes ≤ 40, exactly one shadow-casting light, pixel ratio ≤ 2;
    - the context is not left lost;
    - no frame is requested while idle;
    - GPU memory (`renderer.info.memory`) does not grow;
    - no page error, console error or `THREE.` warning.
  - **Result:** 0 failures over 91 builds and 1 337 renders. Geometries stayed at 14–16, ending at 14–15, and textures at 1, so nothing leaks.
  - **Mutations on the probe:** a no-op `BufferGeometry.dispose` shows at once (14 → 115 geometries in 60 operations), and an extra shadow light fails every operation.
- **My F3 core probe, rerun on today's core** (10 rooms per floor, `access.js`): seeds 1–2, 6 000 operations, 0 failures, every state round-trips exactly.

**Findings**
1. **The architecture held under real WebGL.**
   - The budget is 16 meshes on the sample and 21 on the maximum house (22 with a room selected) against a cap of 40. There is one shadow light and a 1024 map.
   - Rendering is on demand, with no frames while idle or hidden.
   - Context loss and restore rebuild cleanly.
   - Colours follow A-10 (`col` everywhere except the background).
   - My probe adds the one thing the suites did not state directly: over 91 rebuilds there is **no GPU memory leak**.
2. **Running real r128 locally paid for itself.** B-1 (the basement pit covered by `groundOuter`) passed on the mock and failed only on real r128. It is now guarded by QA's magenta leak check, and the TL made a basement house mandatory in level-dependent real-r128 checks. The rule for later work: the mock proves which API is used, real r128 proves what is drawn, and both stay mandatory.
3. **Debt (none blocks):**
   - **FS:** the lint length warnings in `layout.js` (334 lines) and `stage-plans.js` (320) get a split at the next change to those files.
   - **TL list, accepted as is:** S6 near-equivalent mutation, store-only edge states, one `Color` per look, seating tie order.
4. **Run time:** default 398.8 s (≤ 420 s) and `QA_FULL=1` 450.4 s (≤ 480 s), with longest-first scheduling. Accepted. The 480 s cap stays the hard limit.
5. **CD-5 card:** no reason to send it again. The client decided CD-5 ("warn") on 2026-11-21, and the frequency figure (about 1 floor in 4) is information, not a new decision. Put it in the release note's known limits instead of a card.
