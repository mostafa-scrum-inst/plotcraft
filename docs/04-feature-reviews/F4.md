**Accepted limits to restate to the client** (from the outcomes, in his words)
- **The default 3D view looks from the street.** The pool sits behind the house and appears after one drag; the garden seating shows at the back corner.
- **"Way in" warnings (CD-5)** treat any patch of hall as a way in, even a closed pocket that does not connect to the stairs or the entrance.
- **Large gardens:** at most 120 trees and 200 shrubs (about 1 020 m² of planting) are drawn, so very large gardens look thinner than the % asked, with no message.
- **The sun is approximate,** not real solar data, as labelled in the app.
- **Raising the vegetation % can swap some shrubs for trees;** existing trees never move.
- **A full 10-room floor on a large plot is drawn smaller on a phone** (13.2 px/m). Every room still has its label.
- **Rooms in 3D are told apart by floor colour, not by name labels.** Names show in the room finder and on the tour caption (F5).
